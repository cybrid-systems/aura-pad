# M11e — Aura-unique features

Part of [M11](m11.md). Soft tip `c69e644`.

## M11e — time machine + open-is-restore (`soft/pad/time.aura`, `PAD_M11_TIME_OK`)

**What the kid sees.** Every helper KEEP is a step on a strip:

> start | helper: closer | [macro: flip]

**ctrl-t** goes one step back in time, **ctrl-n** one step forward. The
say line says what came back, in kid words:

> back to step 2 of 3: add like before the macro's flip
> back to step 1 of 3: hello and bye like before the helper's closer
> forward to step 2 of 3: the helper's closer is back (hello and bye)

Each step brings back its page and its "who" stamps. A new KEEP after
stepping back starts a new branch and cuts the old future
(`start | [helper: swap]`). ctrl-z on the newest KEEP takes its step off
the strip too.

A book saves and opens again with its story:

> the book is open: 2 saved steps, aura has the whole story

The strip then shows the saved steps first (`helper: closer | macro: flip
| [opened]`). ctrl-o on a row the helper wrote before the save still
answers "the AI helper wrote hello: closer (change 3), from the saved
book".

**How.**
1. `pad:robot-commit!` calls `pad:time-note!` after the KEEP. That takes
   an `ast:snapshot` and stores the step: snapshot, page object, stamps,
   who / why / names, `query:code`, generation. The strip starts at
   `pad:time-start!` (after the check, or after an open).
2. A move to step *i*:
   - first `ast:diff` (step *i*'s snapshot against now) gives two
     canonical programs. Soft splits them into top-level defines and
     keeps the names whose text differs;
   - Soft also collects the names the skipped steps changed;
   - then `ast:restore` + one `eval-current` (aura#4349), and the step's
     page and stamps come back. Robot undo is cleared: the strip is the
     history now.
   - The two name sets must be equal, and `query:code` must equal the
     code saved with the step. Otherwise the move prints
     `M11_TIME_DISAGREE` and fails the smoke.
3. Save (`pad:time-save! base`) writes `base.aw` with
   `serialize-workspace` (code + mutation log) and `base.pad` with
   `write-file`. The `.pad` sidecar holds the page rows as char codes,
   the stamps, and the story (`STEP who names why`).
4. Open (`pad:time-open! base`) runs `deserialize-workspace`, so there is
   no `set-code` and the log comes back with every change reason
   (`sum=`). The page and stamps come from the sidecar, and the strip
   restarts at "opened" with the saved story in front.
5. Who after an open. The reopened log is not usable as it is:
   - every record says `author=0` (aura#4365);
   - the reload renumbers the tree, but log targets and
     `query:node-provenance` keep the old node ids, so `bye` and `add`
     read as "nobody" and the log cannot be joined to a define by id
     (aura#4367, filed).

   So a define last written before the open takes who from its row's pad
   stamp, and aura confirms it: the reopened log must hold a rebind whose
   reason equals the stamp's why (`pad:time-eng-of`). A write after the
   open has a real fingerprint and uses plain M11b. Its mutation id can
   repeat a reopened one, because the id counter is not restored, so ids
   never decide old vs new; the author fingerprint does.
6. aura#4366 (filed): `deserialize-workspace` returns `#f` and skips the
   log when the program's value is a list. The notebook source used to end
   with the rows define (a list), so `pad:ws-source` now ends with a
   trailing `#t`.

**Measured on the tip.**
- `TIME from=3 to=2 eng=add soft=add code=yes verdict=agree`.
- `TIME from=3 to=1 eng=hello.bye.add soft=hello.bye.add code=yes verdict=agree`.
- After save, clobber (`set-code` of a fresh page), and open: code and
  page are the same as at save (`E3_CODE`, `E3_PAGE`).
- `EWHO row=0 name=hello who=helper why=closer ... verdict=agree reopened=yes author=0`.
- A KEEP after the open answers live (`E3_NEW_LIVE`). Stepping back
  lands on the opened code, and one more ctrl-t says "this is where the
  book was opened".

**Limits.** Snapshots live only in the running process, so after an open
the time machine can go back only to the opening. The saved steps are
listed, but they are story only. In Soft, integer `0` is false: the first
`ast:snapshot` id is `0`, so `time.aura` checks it with `number?`. Keys
are on the notebook face (`pad:robot-line!`), not in `play.aura`, so key
latency is unchanged.
